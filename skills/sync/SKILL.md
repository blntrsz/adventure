# Sync

Grill the **send**, not the subject. Who it is for, what you need back, by when.

## Steps

1. Owning lane from TEAM.md. If none, stop. Unowned questions do not get a form.
2. Write a markdown questionnaire. Each item is one decision, with your recommended answer and what hangs off it.
3. Facts you could look up are not questions.
4. State how answers come back (comment, packet, meeting) and the deadline.
5. Packet the owner with a done when of "questionnaire answered".

**Done when** the owner can answer without a call, or the call has a one-page agenda.

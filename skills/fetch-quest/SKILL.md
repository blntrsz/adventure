# Fetch Quest Guide

1. Identify the source, requested deliverable, permitted transformations, destination, and freshness requirement from the ticket.
2. Retrieve from the authoritative source and preserve provenance.
3. Apply only the requested transformation. Validate format, completeness, and integrity against the Outcome.
4. Place the deliverable where the ticket specifies or attach/link it in the Journal.
5. Post source, retrieval time when available, transformation, validation, and limitations.

A Fetch Quest resolves no open-ended research or product design question. Surface such a discovery for an Adventure amendment. Mark the outcome ready for user review and leave the ticket open.

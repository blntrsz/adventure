# Staff

## Steps

1. Read briefs, rfcs, contracts, open packets. List workstreams.
2. Parallel vs serial: shared person, env, or partner team serializes.
3. Every stream has one owner. Unowned streams are risks, not a plan.
4. Irreversible steps: date, rollback, pause rule.
5. EM ship gates: slo, after-hours owner, partner comms.
6. Write a packet to pm and eng. No code.

**Done when** owners, sequence, and irreversible list are in a packet.
